FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Claude writes all the code and the words for you. You make the decisions, and Claude walks you through every click in plain English.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), because custom skills aren't available on the free plan. You'll also need your logo, a few photos and a domain name. GitHub and Cloudflare are free.

Are there monthly fees?

Hosting on Cloudflare's free plan costs nothing. You only pay for your domain name, usually once a year, to wherever you bought it. Free plans are set by those companies and can change.

How long does it take?

It depends on how many pages you want. Many people can plan and build a simple site over a few short sessions, and every phase is a natural place to stop and pick up later.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Ask Claude for the change, upload the new files to GitHub, and your live site updates in about a minute.

Would you rather I built it with you?
