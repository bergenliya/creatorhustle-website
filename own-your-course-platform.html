FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Claude writes every prompt for Lovable, the AI app builder, and walks you through every click in Stripe and your domain settings in plain English. You make the decisions.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (custom skills aren't on the free plan), a paid Lovable plan, a Stripe account, your own domain and a free Resend account for the welcome email. Plus your course content: names, prices, covers, videos and files.

How much does it cost to run?

There are no course platform fees. You pay for your Lovable plan, Stripe's usual card fees on each sale and your domain once a year, and Resend is free to start. Building and changing your platform uses Lovable credits, and my rough estimate is 40 to 80 credits for the core build. Those plans and prices are set by each company and can change.

How long does it take?

Most people need 3 to 5 sessions of about 2 hours for the core platform, plus testing. That's an estimate, and every stage is a natural place to stop and pick up later.

Is it safe to take payments this way?

Payments run through Stripe, logins and data through Lovable Cloud, and emails through Resend. The skill has you test every buying path in Stripe's test mode first, then do one real purchase with your own card before you go live. And Claude never asks for your secret keys in the chat.

What doesn't it include?

Out of the box, there are no affiliates, certificates, community area or mobile app, and taxes and legal pages stay your responsibility, like with any online shop. You can add extras later with more Lovable prompts.
