Introducing

Newsletter Studio

Newsletter Studio learns your voice once, in a 20-minute setup. Then each week Claude asks one question about your week, finds the story in your answer and writes the whole email: subject lines, preview text, the email itself and your sign-off.

And it sells, gently. Your offers show up in the emails you were sending anyway, using seven honest angles that rotate so it never feels samey. It works with whichever email platform you already use.

How it works

Four simple steps

1

Set up your voice, once

Claude asks about your voice, your readers, your offers and your rhythm, then builds a voice profile and a simple shape for your newsletter.

2

Answer one question

Each week, Claude asks what's happened since your last email. A messy, voice-note style answer is perfect.

3

Get the full draft

Subject line with two alternatives, preview text, the email and your sign-off, with this week's selling angle woven in honestly.

4

Tidy, paste and send

Ask for one round of changes, then paste it into your email platform as plain text, with layout notes, or as HTML.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Will it really sound like me?

That's the whole point of the one-time setup. Claude learns your voice from emails or posts you've written (or from you describing your week as if texting a friend), and if a draft ever sounds off, you tell it what rang false and it updates your voice profile, not just that email.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), because custom skills aren't available on the free plan, and the email platform you already use. There's nothing to connect: you paste the finished email in.

Which email platforms does it work with?

Any of them: Kit, MailerLite, Flodesk, Mailchimp, Substack, beehiiv, ActiveCampaign and others. You can have plain text, copy with layout notes, or HTML if your platform accepts it.

Will it make my emails pushy?

No. It rotates seven honest ways to mention your offer, from a story that lands on it to the occasional direct ask, and you choose how much selling feels right. Some weeks it'll suggest no pitch at all.

Does it make things up?

It's built not to. Anything Claude doesn't know, like a number, a client story or a link, goes in [brackets] for you to fill in, and it checks every draft for details you didn't give it.

How long does each newsletter take?

The voice setup takes about 20 minutes, once. After that, most people take a few minutes per send, plus however long they like to spend tweaking.
