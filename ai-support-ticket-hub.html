FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be techy?

Not at all. Claude walks you through every click in plain English, and you can send it a screenshot whenever you're not sure what to do next.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), because custom skills aren't available on the free plan. You'll also set up free Airtable, Fillout and Slack accounts as you go.

How long does it take?

About 90 minutes from start to finish. You can stop after any part and pick it up later.

Are there monthly costs?

The whole system runs on the free plans of Airtable, Fillout and Slack. Free plan limits are set by those companies and can change, so check them when you sign up.

How do I get it?

Straight after checkout you'll get a download with the skill file, a readable copy of the guide and your Start Here PDF.

Can I use it with ChatGPT?

The skill is made for Claude. The readable copy of the guide is included too, so you can follow the steps on your own if you prefer.
