FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Claude writes all the copy and code with you. You review it, click where it tells you, and paste. Every step comes with numbered instructions, and you can send a screenshot whenever you're unsure.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (custom skills aren't on the free plan), a Stripe account, a free Cloudflare account, an email platform like Kit, MailerLite or Flodesk, and your offers: a freebie, a low-priced offer and an upsell. An order bump and a downsell are optional.

My website is on Squarespace, Wix or WordPress. Will it work?

Yes. If you can't add your own pages, Claude puts your funnel on a free subdomain like offers.yourbrand.com with Cloudflare, and you link to it from your main site.

How do one-click upsells work?

Buyers enter their card once on your tripwire page. Stripe saves it securely for that order, so on the upsell and downsell pages they just click yes. A double click never charges twice, and the one-click window closes after 2 hours.

Are there monthly fees?

No funnel software fees. You pay Stripe's normal fees on each sale, and Cloudflare's free plan covers the payment helper for most small businesses.

Is my Stripe account safe?

Your secret Stripe key never goes in the chat. Claude shows you exactly where to paste it in Cloudflare, so it never reaches the page or the browser. Prices are set in your payment helper, so buyers can't change what they pay.

What doesn't it do?

It doesn't include affiliates, payment plans, subscriptions, coupons, automatic sales tax or cart-abandonment emails. If you need those right now, a dedicated checkout tool may suit you better.

How long does it take?

Usually 3 to 4 sessions of about 2 hours, depending on how quickly you review the copy. Every path gets tested in Stripe test mode before you go live.
