FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be techy?

Not at all. I handle the technical side and explain anything you need to know in plain English.

What exactly is an AI skill?

A set of instructions that guides an AI assistant through a specific task. Instead of a generic prompt, your buyer gets a skill that walks them through your own method, like your way of planning a launch.

What if I don't know which skills to create?

That's okay. Share your expertise, your offers and the questions your audience asks most, and I'll help you pick them before I start building. You can also choose skills from my PLR library.

What do my buyers need to use the skills?

A Claude account with Skills turned on.

Where can I sell them?

Anywhere you sell digital downloads, like WordPress, Shopify, Stan Store, Gumroad or ThriveCart.

Can I sell them under my own brand?

Yes. They're built for your business, so you sell them as your own products.
