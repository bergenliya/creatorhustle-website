Questions, answered

What's included?

Every course and Shopify product I sell right now, plus every new course and product I release during your 12 months.

What isn't included?

My services (like done-for-you builds and setups), sponsorships, third-party software subscriptions, and memberships such as the AI-Powered Creators Club and Clone Content Machine.

Will I get every new product you release?

You'll get every new course and digital product I launch during your 12 months. Services and separately announced special offers aren't included.

Can I resell the products?

Some products come with PLR or commercial-use rights, and others are for use in your own business only. Each product's licence tells you what you can change, share or sell.

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. There's something for every level, so you can start with the beginner-friendly resources and work up as you get more confident.

What happens after 12 months?

Your access ends after 12 months. If you'd like to keep using everything and get the next year's new releases, you can renew at a member price.

What does the founding price mean?

$397 is the founding price for your first 12 months. I'll let you know the renewal price before your year ends.
