Do you have a workbook sitting in Google Drive that nobody ever finishes?

Same. I have a whole folder of them :)

You put real care into those PDFs. The questions are good. The exercises work. But people download them, save them to their desktop, and that's where the story ends.

So here's what I've been doing instead. I turn them into an **interactive workbook** that people fill in right on the screen, and it takes about 20 minutes with Claude.

## Why PDF workbooks don't get finished

It's not your content. It's the format.

A PDF asks people to print it, find a pen, and sit down with it. Most of us don't have that kind of time anymore (I build my business in nap times, so believe me, I get it).

An interactive workbook asks for much less. People open a link, type their answers, and they're done. Their answers are saved, so they can close it and come back tomorrow.

And when someone actually finishes your freebie, they remember who made it. That's when they come back to buy from you.

## What you need before you start

You only need three things:

1. **A Claude account** with Skills turned on. Skills are like little instruction manuals you give Claude so it knows exactly how to do a job.
2. **The free PDF to Interactive Workbook skill.** You can [get it here](/pdf-to-interactive-workbook), it comes by email with setup steps.
3. **One PDF you already made.** A workbook, worksheet, planner or checklist all work well.

That's it. No website builder, no developer, no code.

## How to turn your PDF into an interactive workbook

Here's the whole process, step by step.

### Step 1: Add the skill to Claude

Open the email with your skill file and follow the setup steps. It's a one-time thing, and after that the skill is always there when you need it.

### Step 2: Upload your PDF

Start a new chat in Claude and attach the PDF you want to bring back to life.

### Step 3: Type one sentence

Type “Turn this into an interactive workbook.”

Claude reads your questions and exercises and rebuilds them as a workbook people fill in on screen, in your brand style.

### Step 4: Ask for changes

Want different colours? A progress bar? A space for notes at the end? Just ask. You can keep tweaking until it feels like yours.

## What your PDF could become

Here are a few ideas to get you thinking:

- **A product-planning workbook.** Clients answer questions about their audience, compare their ideas and finish with their own product brief.
- **A launch-preparation workbook.** Clients work through your process, check what's ready and see their next steps.
- **A reflection and action workbook.** Clients respond to your coaching questions and turn their answers into a practical plan.

Not sure which one to start with? Try the [Mini App Idea Finder](/mini-app-idea-finder). Answer a few questions and you'll get a ready-to-build plan.

## Where to use your new interactive workbook

Once it's built, you can use it in a few ways:

- **As your freebie.** Swap your old PDF opt-in for the interactive version, so people use it instead of saving it for later.
- **As a course bonus.** Add it to a course or coaching program so clients do the work, not just watch the videos.
- **As a small product.** Sell it on its own. Mini tools like this usually sell somewhere between $9 and $47.
