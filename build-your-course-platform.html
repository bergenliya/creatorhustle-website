FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Claude writes every prompt for Lovable, and Lovable writes the code. You paste the prompt, try the result and tell Claude what you see. Claude walks you through every click in plain English.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), because custom skills aren't available on the free plan. You'll also need a paid Lovable plan, a Stripe account, a free Resend account and a domain you own.

What does it cost to run?

There are no platform fees. Building uses Lovable credits (roughly 40 to 80 for the core platform, an estimate that depends on how many fixes you need), Stripe takes its usual card fees, and you pay for your domain and Lovable plan. Prices are set by those companies and can change.

How long does it take?

Most people need 3 to 5 sessions of about 2 hours, plus testing. First builds often take longer, and every stage is a natural place to stop and pick up later.

Can I add order bumps and upsells?

Yes. Once the core platform works, Claude can add an order bump at checkout, a one-click upsell after purchase, a freebie opt-in connected to your email platform and a review-for-a-gift button.

What doesn't it do?

There's no built-in affiliate programme, certificates, community, mobile app, drip schedule or automatic VAT calculation. Some can be added later with more Lovable prompts, and Claude will tell you honestly what's involved.
