FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be techy?

Not at all. I handle the technical side and explain anything you need to know in plain English.

Are the sessions live?

Yes. Every session is private and live, just you and me, working inside your own accounts.

Do I need to film anything?

A short recording is usually needed to create your clone. I'll explain exactly what to record before we start.

I don't like being on camera. Will this work for me?

Yes, that's exactly who it's for. You record once, and your clone shows up on camera for you from then on.

Do I own my clone?

Yes. Your avatar, voice and scripts live in your own accounts, so they're yours to keep and use.

Should I tell my audience it's an AI clone?

That's your choice, but being open about it usually builds trust. We'll talk about how to introduce your clone to your audience.

Which tools do we use?

The same tools I use for my own content: HeyGen for your video avatar, ElevenLabs for your voice clone, Claude for your scripts, and Higgsfield for extra B-roll and reels.

Do I need to pay for any tools?

Yes, HeyGen, ElevenLabs, Claude and Higgsfield each have their own subscriptions. I'll tell you which plans you need before we start, so there are no surprises.

What happens after the sessions?

You get 4 weeks of private DM support while you start creating with your clone.
