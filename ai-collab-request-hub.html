FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be techy?

Not at all. Claude walks you through every click in plain English, and you can send it a screenshot whenever you're not sure what to press.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), because custom skills aren't available on the free plan. You'll also need free accounts on Airtable, Fillout and Slack, which the skill helps you set up.

Does the AI say yes to collabs for me?

No. It only drafts replies. Good fits get a warm next step, poor fits get a kind no, and anything that needs your decision is flagged at the top of the draft. You read every reply and send it yourself.

Are there monthly fees?

The setup runs on the free plans of Airtable, Fillout and Slack. AI drafts use Airtable's monthly AI allowance. Free plans are set by those companies and can change.

How long does it take?

About 90 minutes, and you can stop after any part and pick it up later.
