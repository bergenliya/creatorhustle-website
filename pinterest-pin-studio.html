Introducing

Pinterest Pin Studio

This Claude Skill is built on the pin system I use for my own shop. Tell it what you want to pin, from products and blog posts to freebies, and it plans a week or a month of pins, rotates your designs so they stay fresh, and writes the words for every single one.

Everything lands in your pin planner in Notion or Google Sheets, so you always know what's planned, scheduled and live. And with the Quick Pin Design bonus, Claude designs the pin images too, no Canva needed.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be techy?

Not at all. The setup guide walks you through every step with copy buttons, and Claude asks you one simple question at a time.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), because custom skills aren't available on the free plan. Plus a free Notion or Google account for your pin planner. Canva is optional.

Do I need a Shopify store?

No. Pin anything with a link: products from any shop, blog posts, freebies, courses, services or podcast episodes.

Does it post to Pinterest for me?

It plans and writes your pins and keeps your planner up to date. You schedule them with Pinterest's own free scheduler or the scheduling tool you already use.

What's the Quick Pin Design bonus?

A second Claude Skill that designs finished pin images for you. Paste a link and it makes 5 pins in your brand colours and fonts, no Canva needed.

How long does setup take?

Around 30 minutes, once. After that, a week of pins takes a few minutes.
