Pinterest Pin Studio

This Claude Skill is the pin system I use for my own Pinterest. You make your pin templates and mockups once in Canva. Claude picks what to promote, fills your templates, rotates them so every pin looks fresh, and writes SEO titles and descriptions in your voice.

Everything lives in a Google Sheet pin database, and every batch shows up in a Notion board so you can check it before anything is scheduled. It works for blog posts, products, courses, freebies and offers.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to design anything new?

No. You use the Canva pin templates and mockups you already have. Claude fills them in with the right image and words for each pin, and makes every pin as a new design so your templates stay untouched.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (custom skills aren't on the free plan), with Canva, Google Drive and Notion connected to Claude. You'll also need your pin templates, your mockups and a free Pinterest business account. Metricool is optional for automatic scheduling.

Is it only for shop owners?

Not at all. It works for anything with a link: blog posts, products, courses, freebies, services and offers. You can mix them all in one pin plan.

How does the rotation work?

Each category's templates rotate in rounds, and each item moves on to its next mockup every time it's pinned. The same post or product never gets the same template and mockup pair twice, so every pin looks fresh.

Will anything go to Pinterest without me seeing it?

No. Every batch waits in your Notion Pin Studio board for your approval first. Only the pins you approve get scheduled.

How do the pins get scheduled?

With Metricool if you use it. If you don't, Claude prepares a file for Pinterest's free bulk upload (up to 200 pins at a time), or a simple checklist to upload them yourself.

How long until I see results?

Pinterest is slow and steady. Results usually build over weeks and months, not days. This system makes showing up consistently easy, which is what Pinterest rewards.
